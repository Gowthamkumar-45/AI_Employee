# Qualification Guide

This file defines how to check whether a lead is a good fit before outreach.

## Qualification checklist

- **Company fit**
  - Is this a startup or software products business?
  - Does the company look like it needs automation, product growth, or software development support?

- **Role fit**
  - Is the contact a founder, co-founder, product lead, growth lead, or decision-maker?
  - Do they have influence over software or product purchase decisions?

- **Contact quality**
  - Is the email valid?
  - Is the phone number available?
  - Is the LinkedIn profile complete and real?
  - Are there any additional social profiles?

- **Realness check**
  - Is the lead real and active?
  - Can the company and contact be verified with Lusha, public records, or profile signals?

- **Need signal**
  - Does the company show signs of product expansion, growth, hiring, or automation needs?
  - Is there a clear pain point we can solve with startupculture products?

## Decision criteria

- **Qualified**: company fits, contact is real, role is decision-making, and there is a clear need signal.
- **Needs more research**: if contact is real but qualification is incomplete.
- **Discard**: if the lead is not a fit, contact is invalid, or the company is not in our target profile.

## Notes

- do not proceed to outreach until the lead is verified real.
- always include the phone number, email, linkedin profile, and available social media profiles when passing the lead forward.
