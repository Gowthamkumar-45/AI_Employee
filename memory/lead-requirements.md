# Lead Requirements

This file defines the requirements and fit criteria for leads.

## Lead requirements

- industry: nail care startups, beauty brands, cosmetics, personal care, or nail wellness products
- company stage: early-stage to growth-stage startups
- business model: product-first companies selling directly to consumers or retailers
- decision-maker: founders, co-founders, product leads, marketing leads, or operations leads
- lead signals: new product launches, international expansion, retail growth, subscription launches, or beauty brand scaling

## What to capture

- company name
- product category
- website or app URL
- target market
- current stage or funding status
- key pain points or product needs

## Use

- use this file when scouting leads to determine whether the company matches our nail care startup requirements
- update the file if our lead criteria changes
- refer to this file before adding any lead to the pipeline
