# Qualify Lead Skill

When a lead is ready for qualification, do this:

## Step 1: Verify company fit
- confirm the company is a startup or software products business
- check if they show product, automation, or growth needs
- note their stage, market, and any product signals

## Step 2: Verify role and decision power
- confirm the contact is founder, co-founder, product lead, growth lead, or buyer
- check if they can influence software or product decisions
- capture their exact role and responsibility

## Step 3: Verify contact information
- confirm email, phone, LinkedIn, and any additional social profiles
- verify the lead is real using Lusha or public signals
- if contact info is missing or invalid, stop and do not proceed to outreach

## Step 4: Score the lead
- fit: is the lead a good startupculture match?
- contact quality: is the info complete and verifiable?
- need signal: do they have a clear product or automation pain?
- timing: are they active, hiring, launching, or raising?

## Step 5: Choose next step
- mark the lead ready for outreach if qualified
- mark as needs research if more verification is required
- discard if the lead is not a fit
- capture the decision and reasoning in memory/pipeline.md
