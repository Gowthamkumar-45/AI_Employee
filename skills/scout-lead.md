# Scout Lead Skill

When you need new startup prospects, do this:

## Step 1: Find target startups
- search Apollo.io, LinkedIn, and other lead sources
- use `memory/lead-requirements.md` to determine fit and lead criteria
- evaluate companies against the lead requirements and look for matching product or growth signals

## Step 2: Identify the decision-maker
- find founders, co-founders, product leads, growth leads, or other purchase influencers
- capture name, role, company, email, phone, LinkedIn, and other social profiles
- collect the lead source and the reason they look like a fit

## Step 3: Verify the lead is real
- use Lusha, public profiles, or company data to confirm the contact
- check the company website, LinkedIn presence, and any available product information
- if the lead cannot be verified, do not pass it forward

## Step 4: Record and pass the lead
- add the lead to memory/pipeline.md with verification notes
- include full contact details and source information
- set the next step: qualification, outreach, or discard
