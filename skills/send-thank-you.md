# Send Thank-You Skill

After a meeting ends, do this:

## Step 1: Review the notes
- use the Fireflies transcript and action items
- identify the key discussion points and questions
- note any agreed next steps or commitments

## Step 2: Draft the thank-you note
- keep the message short, personal, and professional
- thank the lead for their time
- mention one or two specific points from the call

## Step 3: Confirm next steps
- restate the agreed next action clearly
- say what will happen next and when
- make it easy for the lead to reply if needed

## Step 4: Log the follow-up
- send the note via Gmail
- update memory/pipeline.md with the thank-you note and next step
