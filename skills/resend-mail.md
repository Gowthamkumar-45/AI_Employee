# Resend Mail Skill

When a lead has not replied after the waiting period, do this:

## Step 1: Confirm status
- verify the lead received the first-touch outreach
- check that the lead has not replied or taken the next step
- confirm the lead is still a good target

## Step 2: Review the prior message
- read the previous outreach and the lead context
- identify one new point or reminder to add
- avoid repeating the exact same message

## Step 3: Craft the resend email
- write a short, polite follow-up
- reference the prior message briefly
- add one fresh detail, example, or value point
- ask a simple question or a clear next step

## Step 4: Send and log
- send the follow-up via Gmail
- note the resend date and reason in memory/pipeline.md
- update the lead stage and next step

## Rules
- keep follow-up cadence respectful
- do not resend if the lead already replied or the opportunity changed
- do not resend too often
