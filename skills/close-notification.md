# Close Notification Skill

When a lead outcome is final, do this:

## Step 1: Capture the outcome
- confirm whether the lead is closed-won or closed-lost
- record lead name, company, role, email, phone, and linkedin
- note the final status and any key reasons

## Step 2: Summarise the decision
- write a short summary of why the deal closed
- include objections, missing fit, or reasons for win
- add any next steps or handoff details

## Step 3: Notify admin
- send the summary via Slack or the designated admin channel
- keep it brief and clear
- include the final status, lead details, and next action

## Step 4: Update the pipeline
- update memory/pipeline.md with the close status
- note whether the opportunity is won or lost
- capture any future follow-up or referral actions
